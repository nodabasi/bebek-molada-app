Terms of Use

A safe map for everyone

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Bebek Molada for iOS and Android

In short

The spots and notes in Bebek Molada are added by parents. By using the app you accept these terms. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for users who post it.

What you share

  • Only add real, baby-friendly places, with an accurate name, facilities and location.
  • Do not post profanity, insults, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexual content, violence, advertising or spam in notes or in your profile name.
  • Do not share other people's personal information.
  • You are responsible for what you post. It is shown to other users together with your name.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

We remove spots and notes that break these rules and may close the accounts of users who break them, without notice. The app also blocks text that contains objectionable words from being posted.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report an objectionable or wrong spot or note from the ⋯ menu at the top right of the spot card, or the ⋯ button next to a note.
  • We review reports within 24 hours, remove the content when needed and act on the user who posted it. Content reported by several different people is hidden right away, before review.
  • From the same menu you can block the person who posted it. You will no longer see their spots and notes. You can unblock them in Settings.

Accuracy

Information on the map comes from users and open sources. We cannot guarantee that a place is open or has the listed facilities; please check with the venue.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings tab. See Account Deletion and the Privacy Policy.

Changes

If these terms change materially, the app asks you to accept the new version once.
