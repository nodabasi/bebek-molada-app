Bebek Molada

Out with your baby, the nearest break spot in your pocket

Stop hunting for a changing table

Out with a baby, the question is always the same: where is the nearest baby room? Bebek Molada puts the places in Istanbul where you can nurse, change a nappy or warm a bottle on a single map, on both the Asian and European sides. Malls, hospitals, parks, cafés and more.

What's on the map

  • Baby care room
  • Nursing area
  • Bottle warming
  • Pushchair
  • Play area
  • Family toilet

Filter the map by what you need and see only the spots that offer it.

How it works

  • See what's nearbyAllow location and the map centres on you, showing the break spots around you.
  • Get directionsTap a spot to open walking directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
  • Add a new spotFound a baby room that isn't listed? Add it from the map so other parents can use it too.
  • Still there? Confirm itWhen you visit a spot, confirm it and leave a short note. Spots with more confirmations are more reliable.

From parents, for parents

We researched and added some of the spots ourselves; parents add the rest and keep them current. Every spot shows who added it and when it was last confirmed.

Good to know

  • Free, supported by ads, no in-app purchases
  • Sign up with email; the address must be verified
  • Your location is only used to centre the map and is never saved on our servers
  • Delete your account from inside the app at any time
  • Currently Istanbul only, in Turkish
