Deleting your Bebek Molada account and data

From inside the app

  1. Open Bebek Molada and sign in.
  2. Go to the Ayarlar (Settings) tab at the bottom.
  3. Tap Hesabımı Sil (delete my account).
  4. In the confirmation dialog, tap Evet, sil (yes, delete).

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. If the app says too much time has passed since you last signed in, sign out, sign back in and try again.

If you can't get into the app

From the email address registered to your account, send a message with the subject “Bebek Molada account deletion” to odabasi.developer@gmail.com.

Once we have confirmed the request comes from the account owner, it is completed within 30 days and you are notified by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details (email address and password)
  • Your profile: your name and age
  • Your children's names and birth dates

These are deleted permanently and cannot be recovered.

What stays

Break spots you added to the map and confirmation notes you left are part of the shared map, so they remain after your account is deleted, still showing the name that was on your profile at the time.

If you want those deleted too, or your name removed from them, write to the address above and we will do it within the same period. Writing before you delete your account makes it easier for us to find which records are yours.

Advertising data collected by our ad partner Google is held by Google, not by us. You can manage it from your device's ad settings; see the Privacy Policy.
